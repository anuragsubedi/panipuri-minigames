How Glimpse works

A cloud of dots flashes for a moment and you guess how many there were. It tests your sense of quantity, so the score cares about how many times off you are, not how many dots.

Play Glimpse

A round

  1. A short "get ready" pause (1.5 s) so you're looking.
  2. The dots flash for 1 s on Easy or 0.4 s on Hard: too quick to count.
  3. You type your guess (1–999) and see the real number.

How many dots

Where the dots go

Dots are placed at random, but a new dot is only kept if it's far enough from every other one. Dots never touch or hide behind each other, so what you see is really all there is.

Scoring: how many times off

Guessing 11 when there were 10 is as good as guessing 110 for 100. And guessing double is as wrong as guessing half. A ratio error gives both:

error = | ln(guess / actual) |

points = 10 × e−(error / 0.42)2
